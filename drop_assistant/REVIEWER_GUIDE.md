# Simple review interface — version 2.1

Each card has Name, Reason, and optional Additional info. Expand Additional evidence and match details to inspect comparisons and provenance.

For Sofia, Keep data approves the exemption. Reject synthetic exemption & delete removes the exemption and deletes the synthetic record in one audited transaction. Enter why the exemption should be rejected. This override requires synthetic source provenance.

AI candidates still require an accept/deny choice. Dispute and Defer keep the consumer request unresolved; follow-up is assigned to the entered name, due today. Reopen makes a held case available for a separate decision.

Extract into a new folder to avoid replacing a working database. To retain earlier work, stop the app, back up its data folder, and copy its SQLite files into the new data folder. Older records without synthetic provenance cannot use the exemption override.
